Revision notes

Key content from your three Unit 5 lessons. Select a topic or read them all. Expand the examination tips to support your explanations.

Ethical and cultural issues

Think about who gains from new technology and who may lose out. Consider equality, consent, fairness, privacy, social change and jobs.

Environmental issues

The lifecycle of a device includes mining, manufacture, transport, use, repair or upgrading, and disposal or recycling.

Legislation and privacy

Distinguish the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR, Computer Misuse Act 1990, and Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
