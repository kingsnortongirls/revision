Interactive revision tools

Investigate the processes behind the theory, not just the definitions.

Visualise scheduling algorithms

Four fresh CPU-only jobs arrive at different times: Aurora (0, 5), Breeze (1, 3), Cedar (3, 2) and Dune (4, 4). Values are (arrival time, CPU burst). Compare completion, waiting and response times.

Teaching model: all jobs are CPU-only, a context switch costs 0 time units, FCFS/SJF are non-preemptive, and arrivals at the same time use listed order. The illustrative MLFQ uses two queues (2- and 4-tick quanta), higher queue first and demotion after exhausting a quantum; it is one possible MLFQ policy, not a universal definition.

Virtual page to physical frame

Choose a virtual address, then follow the page table. This model uses a page size of 256 bytes and a fixed 8-entry page table.

Segment base and bounds

Unlike fixed-size pages, segments may have different lengths. Enter an offset and see whether it is valid.

Interrupt priority and LIFO stack

Step through an audio-mixing controller. A routine is interrupted by an ordinary network event; a higher-priority safety signal interrupts the service routine.
